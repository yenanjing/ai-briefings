ScienceBuddy: Recursive-in-Recursive Self-Improvement for Interactive Scientific Agents
👥 Shuhan Xue, Jianyuan Zhong, Ziyuan Nan, Wenbin Li, Zhaochen Yu, Jinchao Ding et al. 📄 arXiv:2609.17523
